This policy explains how Daydream handles your information when you explore cities, save destinations, or use Google-powered maps and place details.

## On your device

Daydream stores your saved cities and app preferences in local iOS app storage. I don’t operate a Wishlist account or a server that receives your saved list. Opening a saved city still requests its place information from Google.

You can remove saved cities in Wishlist. Deleting the app removes its local app data. Apple device backups may include app data according to your backup settings; see [Apple’s iCloud Backup information](https://support.apple.com/en-us/108770).

On supported devices, Daydream generates city summaries using Apple’s on-device Foundation Models language model. Daydream does not send those prompts to an AI server operated by me.

## Google Maps and Places

Daydream uses the Google Maps SDK and Google Places SDK to display maps, city photos, search suggestions, sights, eateries, and place information. Discovery cards request content when they load, including when you first open the app.

When you browse or search, Google receives the requests needed to provide those features. Depending on what you do, those requests can include search text, place identifiers, map areas, and the device coordinates described below. Google also processes SDK and network information, such as IP addresses and request metadata.

The Google SDK privacy manifests included in the current app declare these data types:

| Data type | Purpose | Linked to you or your device |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Device ID | Analytics and app functionality | Yes |
| Other data types | Analytics | Yes |
| Crash data | Analytics | No |
| Performance data | Analytics | No |
| Product interaction | Analytics | No |

These SDK declarations do not identify the data as used for tracking. Daydream does not show ads or add advertising tracking. Data linked to a device can still be linked to you even though Daydream has no account system.

Google uses service data to operate, support, secure, and improve its products. Google’s [Maps Platform data collection and retention information](https://developers.google.com/maps/security/compliance/security-compliance) explains that requests are logged and that retention varies. I don’t set Google’s retention periods or control deletion of its service logs. Clearing app data does not delete information already received by Google.

When you use Google Maps features and content in Daydream, the [Google Maps End User Additional Terms of Service](https://maps.google.com/help/terms_maps/) and [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy) apply. You can also read [Google’s guidance on SDK privacy disclosures](https://developers.google.com/maps/documentation/ios-sdk/apple-privacy-policy).

## Location is optional

With your permission, Daydream can use your location to find the city near you. When you use this feature, your device coordinates are sent to Google in a Nearby Search request. Depending on your iOS settings, the location can be precise or approximate.

Daydream requests location access while the app is in use. It does not request background location access, and I don’t operate a server that stores your location history.

You can change or revoke location access in iOS Settings under Privacy & Security → Location Services → Daydream. You can continue to explore cities with search or shuffle without granting location permission. Google’s privacy and retention practices apply to the location-related requests it receives.

## Support email

If you email [daydreamiosapp@gmail.com](mailto:daydreamiosapp@gmail.com), I receive your email address, your message, and any attachments you choose to send. I use that information to reply and help with your request. Email is also processed by the email providers involved in delivering it.

I keep support correspondence as needed to handle the request and any applicable recordkeeping obligations. You can contact me with questions about that correspondence or request its deletion, subject to those obligations.

## This website and external links

This website does not add analytics scripts, advertising, or tracking cookies. It is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub [logs visitor IP addresses for security](https://docs.github.com/en/pages/getting-started-with-github-pages/what-is-github-pages), and its [Privacy Statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement) applies to its hosting services.

Links to the App Store, Google Maps, reviews, or other websites may open services operated by other companies. Their privacy policies apply when you use those services.

## Your choices

- **Location:** change the app’s permission in iOS Settings, or use search and shuffle instead.
- **Wishlist:** remove saved cities in the app. Apple’s backup settings control whether app data is included in device backups.
- **Cached place data:** use the app’s “Clear cached data” control. This is separate from removing saved cities in Wishlist.
- **Support information:** email me with a privacy question or a request concerning information you have sent me.

Daydream is a general-audience travel discovery app and is not designed specifically for children. If you have a concern about a child’s information, please contact me.

## Security and policy updates

I rely on iOS app storage protections and the services’ security measures, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

I may update this policy as Daydream or its services change. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated. Daydream’s App Store privacy details are also available on its [App Store page](https://apps.apple.com/app/id1366747814).
